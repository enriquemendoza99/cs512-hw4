# CS 412/512 HW4 - Soccer Goalie
Open hw4.html in a browser.

CONTROLS
- Sliders: body position, shoulder, elbow and wrist angles
- Penalty button: shoots the ball to a random spot. Catch it with a glove.

1. HIERARCHY
Body (root) -> Upper arm -> Forearm -> Glove
Two arms, both children of the body.

2. MOVEMENTS
All 4 levels move: the body slides, and the shoulder, elbow and wrist rotate.
UI: 4 sliders and 1 button.

3. BONUS
- Penalty game: catch the ball with a glove (SAVED! or GOAL!)
- Phong lighting (ambient + diffuse + specular)
